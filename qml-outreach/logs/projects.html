QML Project and Repository Archive

These repositories document the framework, teaching activities, algorithms, and applied experiments I have built around the CAIR Quantum Machine Learning division.

Ownership: I am the sole owner and author of the QML work linked on this page. The collaborative Think AI Symposium poster is credited separately on its own page.

Main Framework

  • Charlotte-AI-Research/qml_torch - the main QML Torch framework and group repository.

Teaching Activities

  • simransummermalik/qml-torch-activity-one - feature maps, the two-moons dataset, and the bridge from classical representation learning into QML.
  • simransummermalik/qml-torch-activity-twos - the second QML Torch student activity.

Algorithms and Applied Experiments

  • simransummermalik/grovers - Grover's algorithm work.
  • simransummermalik/QuantumCNN - quantum convolutional neural network experiments.
  • simransummermalik/quantum-tsp - quantum work on the traveling salesperson problem.
  • simransummermalik/quantum-xor - a quantum XOR experiment.

Evaluation Standard

These projects are not meant to treat "quantum" as a performance claim by itself. The larger QML Torch goal is to compare quantum and hybrid methods against classical baselines, identify where quantum structure is actually useful, and make those experiments understandable and reproducible for students.
