Week 10 Spatial Computing Research Log

Date

April 30, 2026

Consolidation

I consolidated the project into a stronger research architecture for live scene reconstruction and algorithmic reasoning. The system needs to remember what it has sensed, stabilize that information into a usable abstraction, and allow BFS, Dijkstra, or A* to query the model without being disrupted by every small geometry update.

Final Research Direction

The work began as a comparison of spatial representations and pathfinding behavior. It developed into a broader systems question about how an immersive application constructs and maintains a decision-relevant model over time.

The offline point-cloud results established a controlled baseline for graph construction and search. The live Vision Pro direction added the harder problems of partial observability, model sufficiency, stability, update timing, and event-driven re-planning.

Next Engineering Step

The next step is to implement the persistent spatial-state layer inside the visionOS application, connect it to a simplified decision-level representation, and test future pathfinding and reachability behavior as that representation changes.
