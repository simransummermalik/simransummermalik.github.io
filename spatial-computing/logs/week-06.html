Week 06 Spatial Computing Research Log

Dates

April 6, 2026 - April 12, 2026

Perception, Modeling, and Reasoning

I formalized three separate but interacting layers for the live system. Perception provides raw reconstruction updates. Modeling converts those updates into persistent structured state. Reasoning operates over the abstraction that best preserves the information needed for a decision.

Algorithms Over Evolving Models

BFS, Dijkstra, and A* were no longer treated as algorithms that wait for a finished graph. I began considering how their outputs might change when the representation is revised over time. This expanded the work from ordinary shortest-path search into search under evolving representational conditions.

Coarse Intermediate Representations

Higher fidelity is not always better. A very detailed graph may fluctuate whenever reconstruction changes, while a coarser occupancy or region-based model may support earlier and more stable decisions. I explored using such a model as a bridge between live geometry and later graph generation.

Engineering Milestone

The immediate milestone became a component that stores reconstruction-derived state over time and supports conversion into a decision-level structure. I continued treating the existing immersive prototype as the systems foundation for this work rather than as a throwaway demonstration.

Offline and Live Roles

The offline point-cloud experiments remained useful for controlled abstraction tests. The Vision Pro application was necessary for studying how the model is constructed, stabilized, and queried in real time. These two tracks were complementary rather than redundant.
