Week 05 Spatial Computing Research Log

Dates

March 30, 2026 - April 5, 2026

Persistent State Manager

I translated the live-modeling direction into a more concrete software target: a persistent spatial-state manager inside the visionOS app. This component would accumulate reconstruction data over time, preserve it in a stable internal structure, and make it available to later abstraction and pathfinding components.

Staged Internal Pipeline

I separated the runtime into four responsibilities:

  1. Receive reconstruction input.
  2. Aggregate and normalize persistent spatial state.
  3. Promote stable information into a decision-level abstraction.
  4. Allow graph and pathfinding algorithms to query that abstraction.

Multiple Coexisting Models

A single representation was unlikely to satisfy every requirement. A geometry-heavy model preserves fidelity but is expensive and volatile. A graph or region-based model is easier to search but necessarily removes detail. I therefore began treating layered, coexisting representations as a core architectural requirement.

Graph Maintenance and Decision Timing

I compared full graph recomputation against selective local revision. I also began framing pathfinding as a model-sufficiency problem: the system may have only a partial environment model but still possess enough information for a local decision. This made the timing of re-planning part of the research question.

Literature Direction

Reading on incremental mapping, partially observed environments, and real-time model updates reinforced the importance of maintaining a representation that is both computationally tractable and relevant to the decisions being made.
